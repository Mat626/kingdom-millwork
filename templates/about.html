The Beginning

Twenty-Five Years of Relentless Craftsmanship

Kingdom Millwork began in 1999 as a specialized bench-joinery shop in Costa Mesa. Founder Marcus Vance recognized that the construction industry had largely abandoned authentic woodworking principles in favor of mass-produced, flat-pack cabinetry and hollow trim.

He set out to restore true guild mastery: hand-selecting kiln-dried North American hardwoods, milling custom blade profiles in-house, and refusing to allow any synthetic filler or inferior substrate into the workshop.

Today, under California State Contractor License LIC# 1150491, Kingdom Millwork operates a state-of-the-art 12,000 sq. ft. fabrication facility and finish shop, partnering with top architects, interior designers, and homeowners across coastal Southern California.
